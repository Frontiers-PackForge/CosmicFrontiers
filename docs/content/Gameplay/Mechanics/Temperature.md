<span style="font-size:35px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> Body Temperature </span> 

<span style="font-size:20px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> Your natural enviroment will impact your body temperature which is indicated by the small round globe. </span> <br>
 This is located near your XP Level. <br>
 When you are cooling down you will see a blue arrow moving dowards. <br>
 When you are heating up you will see an orange arrow moving upwards. <br>
 
 When your body temp icon changes to a Snowflake or Fire - This means your body temp is dangerously cold or hot!
 Try to find somewhere safe that is either warmer or colder than you are currently!
 
 
<span style="font-size:20px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> Body Temperature is affected by </span> 
 
- The Time of Day
- The Season - Winter and Summer being the harshest
- Blocks nearby - Some blocks are hot!
- Being outside or indoors - having a roof or insulated space helps
- Being Dry or Wet - Splashing yourself in water cools you heavily! 

<span style="font-size:20px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> The Sewing Table allows armor insulators to be applied. </span> <br> 
<span style="font-size:15px; color:white"> These can make or break your survival  chances! </span> 

- Cooling Coat - Heat Resistance
- Warming Coat - Cold Resistance
- Thermal Coat - Thermal Resistance (Resists both hot/cold)

<span style="font-size:35px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> General Advice </span> 

- Certain foods and Drink will raise/lower your body temp
- Certain armors help you massively resist temperatures. Check out Snow and Desert armors!
- Certain blocks like magma blocks can be easily found earlygame in ravines or yellowstone biomes, and can be spammed around you to heat up if you are freezing to death.
- The height of the world matters a lot! Being at Y70 or Y160 can easily make a world of a difference!
- Space, and by proxy most planets, is very hot and cold. You'll need to use Nanosuit, Quarktech, or Sanguine Warptech Armor to survive!
- Being in the rain is in most cases **lethal** in the cold seasons, as it drops your body temp by a whole 15C!
