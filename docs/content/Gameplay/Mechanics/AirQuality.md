<span style="font-size:35px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> Air Quality </span> 

<span style="font-size:20px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> Where you are determines the quality of the air. </span> <br>

If you see bubbles like you're drowning while not in water, it probably means that the air around you is no breathable.
Put on your respirator or get out of there to avoid death! 

<span style="font-size:20px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> Your air will be affected by </span> <br>

- Your Y level <br>
    - Below Y = 0 and above Y = 128, you will slowly lose air
- The dimension you're in <br>
    - The you slowly lose air in the Nether
    - There is no air at all in the End
- Drowned will choke air out of your lungs!

<span style="font-weight:bold"> Safety Lanterns change color depending on the air. It works both in your
inventory and in the world. Use it if you're unsure of the air quality! </span>

<span style="font-size:20px; font-weight:bold; color:white"> How to breathe 101: </span> <br>

- Drink a potion of free breathing (renamed from water breathing)
- Use a respirator
- Take a breath using an Air Bladder
- You can create a small pocket of breathable air with Soul Fire, Soul Toches, or Soul Campfires
